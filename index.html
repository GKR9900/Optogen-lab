Make a neuron light-sensitive

An opsin is a light-sensitive protein. If a neuron makes it, light can move ions across the membrane. Scientists insert the opsin gene only into the cell type they want.

Press the button.

Light switch lab

1. Pick an opsin. 2. Press the light button. Watch the voltage graph. Depolarization means the inside gets less negative. Hyperpolarization means it gets more negative.

Opsin summary: ChR2 lets cations (mainly Na⁺) in → excites. eNpHR pumps Cl⁻ in → inhibits. Arch pumps H⁺ out → inhibits. Colours are approximate.
